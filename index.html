About the Film

After the events of the multiverse, Peter Parker tries to return to normal life. But when a mysterious villain appears in New York with technology capable of rewriting reality, Spider-Man will be forced to make a choice that will change everything. Old allies, new enemies, and a mystery rooted in the past—all this awaits the hero in the new chapter of his story.
